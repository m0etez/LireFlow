LireFlow Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 2024

Overview

LireFlow is designed with your privacy in mind. We believe your data belongs to you, and we've built LireFlow to respect that principle.

Data Collection

What We Do NOT Collect

LireFlow does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers:

  • We do not collect analytics or usage data
  • We do not track your reading habits
  • We do not collect device identifiers
  • We do not use advertising networks
  • We do not share any data with third parties

Data Stored Locally

All data is stored exclusively on your device:

  • RSS Feeds: Your subscribed feeds and URLs
  • Articles: Fetched article content and metadata
  • Reading Lists: Your custom lists and saved articles
  • Folders: Your organizational folder structure
  • App Settings: Preferences in local config file
~/Library/Application Support/LireFlow/config.json

Network Requests

LireFlow makes network requests only for:

  • RSS Feed Fetching: Downloading articles from feeds you subscribe to
  • Weather (Optional): Using Open-Meteo API for weather data
  • Feed Icons: Fetching favicons from websites
  • Europresse (Optional): Opening institutional portals in your browser

Location Data

Location is requested only for the weather widget:

  • Used solely for local weather information
  • Sent to Open-Meteo as coordinates only
  • Never stored permanently or shared
  • Optional - you can disable or set manually

Third-Party Services

Service Purpose Privacy
Open-Meteo Weather data (optional) Terms

Data Portability

  • Export: Export feeds, folders, and reading lists anytime
  • Import: Import from JSON or OPML files
  • Delete: Uninstalling removes all local data

GDPR Rights

Since LireFlow stores all data locally and doesn't process personal data on external servers, GDPR rights are automatically fulfilled by the local-only architecture.

Summary

LireFlow is privacy-first:

  • All data stored locally on your device
  • No accounts required
  • No tracking or analytics
  • No data sold to third parties
  • Full data export capability
  • Open source for transparency
